About Us

DRE

Computer Center

Dre Computer Center is your go-to destination for all things tech. Established in 2010, we have been committed to providing top-notch computer products and services to our community. Our mission is to make technology accessible and affordable for everyone.

At Dre Computer Center, we pride ourselves on our extensive selection of laptops, desktops, accessories, and peripherals from leading brands. Our knowledgeable staff is always ready to assist you in finding the perfect device to suit your needs and budget.

Whether you're a student, professional, or tech enthusiast, we have something for you. From the latest laptops and accessories to expert repair services, Dre Computer Center is here to help you stay connected and productive.

Our Services

Computer Repair

Expert diagnostics and repair for laptops and desktops, fixing hardware and software issues to get your device back to peak performance.

Hardware Upgrades

Boost your device’s speed and storage with professional upgrades, including RAM, SSDs, and graphics cards, tailored to your needs.

Software Installation

Hassle-free installation of operating systems, applications, and drivers, ensuring optimal setup and security for your device.

Tech Consultation

Personalized advice to help you choose the right devices, accessories, or solutions for your home, office, or gaming setup.

Data Recovery

Professional recovery services to retrieve lost or corrupted data from hard drives, SSDs, and other storage devices.

Computer Set-up

We provide complete computer setup and configuration for offices, gaming stations, and personal use.
